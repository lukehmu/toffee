Toffee is a COLRv1 variable font with axes wght 300–900, slnt −12–0 and SHDW 0–1000.
  Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  The outline and shadow are colour layers: Chrome and Edge 98+ and Firefox 107+ draw them in full colour (COLRv1).
  Safari and iOS don't support COLRv1; they draw the colours only from COLRv0 layers, and show the plain face without
  them. Choosing a palette with font-palette needs Chrome or Edge 101+, Firefox 107+ or Safari 15.4+.
